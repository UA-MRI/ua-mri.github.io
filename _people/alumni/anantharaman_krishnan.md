Anantha Raman Krishnan earned an M.S. in ECE from the University of Arizona in 2007 and a Ph.D. in ECE in 2011. He is a Principal Engineer in WiFi Systems Engineering at Broadcom Inc. His research background includes signal processing, communication theory, coding and information theory, data storage, biomedical imaging, and compressed sensing.
