Rajagopalan Sundaresan earned an M.S. in ECE from the University of Arizona in 2011. He is a Ph.D. student at the University of Sheffield, focusing on model-based image reconstruction for hyperpolarized <sup>129</sup>Xe MRI, while continuing as a Staff Software Engineer at GE HealthCare. His work includes MRI reconstruction, image processing, compressed sensing, deep learning, and dictionary learning.
