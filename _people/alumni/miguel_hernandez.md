Miguel Hernández-Cabronero earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona in 2015. He was a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Arizona from 2017 to 2018 and is now an Associate Professor at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. His research focuses on image coding, data compression, medical imaging, and remote sensing.
