Robert Crandall earned his Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Arizona in 2017, advised by Ali Bilgin. He is currently a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon Ring AI, where he works on computer-vision applications. His doctoral research focused on nonlocal and randomized methods in sparse signal and image processing.
